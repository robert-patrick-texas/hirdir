# hirdir 0.6.1

Corrections to 0.6.0. A contract says what its agreement would give beside what it
overrides, its earlier terms keep the dates they had, an item on a purchase line is of
the line's model, and the list for the vendor of what is out of service and still
counted can be sorted, filtered and exported. An agreement has a purchase history of
its own.

## New

- **An agreement's purchase history.** The terms an agreement had before it was
  renewed, each with its number, its period, its cost, its purchase reference and who
  renewed it and when, are listed on its page, by `GET /agreements/{hid}/terms` and by
  `hirdir agreements show`, as a contract's are.
- **What 0.6.0 let onto a purchase line of another model is reported.** An item on a
  line of another model than the line's is marked, `purchase_line_mismatch` finds it
  in the item list, and each line counts its own, `mismatched_items`, to be put right.

## Changed

- **An override says what it is in place of.** Each field a contract may have from its
  agreement says, beside the value in force and where it came from, what the agreement
  gives for it, `from_agreement`. The web UI no longer reads the agreement to show it.
  An own value that is the same as the agreement's is still an override.
- **An earlier term has the dates it had.** A contract with no dates of its own is a
  member of its agreement and shares its period, and its earlier terms showed blank
  dates. Each now has the dates of the agreement's term that was in force when it
  began, read from the audit trail, and says which came from the agreement. Its page
  and `hirdir contracts show` mark them.
- **An item on a purchase line is of the line's model.** One that is put on a line and
  names no model, by hand or by a file, takes the line's; one that names another is
  refused, and the answer says the line's model. A file row that names a line and a
  serial number and no model is matched by the line's model, and inherits by it. The
  model of an item is not changed while it is on a line: it is taken off first.
- **The contracts that still count an item out of service are a field.**
  `out_of_service_contracts` on an item's record, `out_of_service_contract.number` and
  `out_of_service_contract.hid` in the item list, and a column of the file of items.
  The coverage report's list for the vendor sorts, filters and exports by it. A
  contract counts the item while its cover goes on past today, or while the term runs
  in which its coverage was ended because the item left.
- **`make upgrade` downloads no upgrade tool.** It asks the registry for each npm
  dependency's latest release through the npm that builds the web UI, writes the new
  versions into `web/package.json`, and installs them together.

## Fixes

- **An item with no lifecycle state** was left out of `out_of_service_cover:eq:false`
  and of every negative test of the contracts that still count it. It is found.
- **The item grid is faster at 50,000 items.** A list is counted from the rows its
  filter matches alone, a section of the coverage report is counted without reading a
  page of it, blank values sort last in one term, and the agreements of a page are read
  once. The grid's first page went from 458 to 173 ms, the list with no filter from 645
  to 216 ms, and the coverage report from 2.8 to 0.8 s, at the median.
- **A stale edit that a term explains says so.** An edit of a contract or an agreement
  refused because its next term has begun no longer says only that someone else
  changed it.

## Additional Notes

- Every component is at its latest production release. Nothing is held back.
- **Upgrading from 0.6.0 changes nothing that is held**: there is no migration, and the
  schema stays at revision `0008`. What reads differently is listed in "From 0.6.0 to
  0.6.1" in [Install and operate](../install.md#from-060-to-061): the earlier terms of
  a contract that shares its agreement's period, the renewals entered with 0.6.0 among
  them, and the new fields. An item that 0.6.0 let onto a purchase line of another model
  stays as it was, is reported, and is put right by giving it the line's model.
- Going back to 0.6.0 needs no backup, since no schema changed: the one
  `apt install --allow-downgrades` command, and what was entered since is kept.
- The package test carries one `SKIP`: with no migration there is none to make fail,
  so that check is skipped and says so. The pass upgrades and goes back all the same.
- **Timing.** `make fixture` fills an inventory of 50,000 fictional items and
  `make time-filters` times the item list on it. The target is now said exactly
  (D-165): the median of 15 requests under 300 ms and the 95th percentile under
  600 ms, on an idle host. The grid's first page and the list with no filter meet it;
  the coverage filters and the sorts by a coverage column do not yet, and are left to
  the timing of 1.0.0 with their numbers, in "Timing at scale" in
  [Architecture](../architecture.md#timing-at-scale).
- **Left to 0.7.0.** An earlier term of a member that was renumbered before its
  agreement's new term began shows the old period, and one of a contract that joined
  or left the agreement partway through is dated by when it began. 0.7.0 renews an
  agreement with the new numbers of its members, each waiting as a next term (D-164).
- P-111 to P-114 are answered and are D-159 to D-162 in [Decisions](../decisions.md),
  two of them changed by their answers. D-163 to D-165 came from the two reviews of
  the server and from the timing.
- **For those who build `hirdir`:** the server was reviewed twice by readers who had
  not written it, the second time after the answers were built; the browser scenarios
  of 0.6.0 that only unit tests held are now specs, run on the build host; and
  `make upgrade` downloads no tool to move the npm dependencies.
- Scenarios S01 to S35, S39 and S40 pass.
