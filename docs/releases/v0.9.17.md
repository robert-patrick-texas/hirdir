# hirdir 0.9.17

Agents: accounts that decide what to change, answerable to a person, held to that
person's role and never Admin, and marked in the audit trail. A user's page says where
each of its roles comes from, and a change that would leave somebody with no role says
so first. The readings of 0.9.14 and 0.9.15 are settled with the owner, the audit trail
holds what a later sync to IPAM, DNS and monitoring will read, and Export everything
loads whole into an empty inventory.

## New

- **Agents** (P-316). A third kind of account, beside a person and a service: a service
  does a fixed job, an agent decides what to change, as a person does. An Admin makes
  one, on New user with Kind Agent or with
  `hirdir admin create-user --kind agent --for USERNAME`, and names the person answerable
  for it. It holds tokens and no access code or session; a role through a group or of
  its own, but never Admin, and never more than its person's role at each request; and
  while its person holds no role, is disabled or is retired, its tokens are refused. Its
  page names its person, the role it holds and the one it is held to, and hands it to
  another person; a person's page lists their agents; a person's retirement says what
  becomes of each, retired or handed over. Every change an agent makes is in the audit
  trail for its person (`agent-reconcile for sam`), and the Audit page shows the changes
  of agents together.
- **Where a role comes from** (P-307). A user's page, and the list of users, say each role
  the account holds: of its own, or through which group, a link. Before an Admin revokes a
  grant, takes somebody out of a group, revokes a group's grant or deletes a group, the
  page says whom it leaves with no role, that each is signed out at once, and whose
  agents' tokens it stops (P-314).
- **A warning for a name another contact has** (P-315). Saving it goes through; the
  contact's page and the Profile say that another contact is named so and that a file
  then tells them apart by the email or the HID, with a link to them.
- **A short browser check through nginx** (P-309): `packaging/package-test.sh
  --browser-check` signs in under the prefix, reads a list, downloads it and opens a page
  by its address, in about two minutes. An ordinary release runs it in place of every
  browser scenario against the package.

## Changed

- **The kind of an account** (P-316, migration `0018`): `kind`, `person`, `service` or
  `agent`, in place of `is_service_account`, in the API, the users' file and the CLI;
  `hirdir admin create-user --kind service` in place of `--service-account`. Every
  service account becomes `service` and every other account `person`.
- **`hirdir admin create-user` refuses a person with no role** (P-313) unless `--no-role`
  is given: no `--role` and no `--group` that grants one is exit `4`, nothing made;
  `--role` with `--no-role` is exit `2`.
- **The readings of 0.9.14 and 0.9.15, as the owner answered them** (P-294 to P-303,
  P-314): most stand as built; the button that confirms a retirement names each record it
  changes (`Release KL and retire contact Kim Lee`), the list of users that are not
  retired is Active users, and a token made for an account that holds no role says it is
  refused until the account holds one.
- **Files keep their HIDs, and may hold the retired** (P-311). A row whose HID no record
  has makes the record with that HID, where it was rejected, unless it is not an HID
  that `hirdir` makes, is or was the HID of a record of any table, or the row's own key
  finds another record; a row that makes a record, and gives its HID, may make it
  retired, with what a retired record keeps; and a record that is not retired may name a
  retired one where `hirdir` keeps such a link after a retirement, as an item its model,
  as the owner chose. The preview says so of each such row. A file still never retires a
  record that is there. Device groups load before items. So Export everything, retired
  records and what runs on a stack or a cluster among them, loads whole into an empty
  inventory and exports the same again.
- **The audit trail for a later sync** (P-310). Every event of an address says the
  address and whose it is, and a retirement or a bringing back says the names and the
  addresses of the record, so that each change to a name, a host name, an FQDN or an
  address of an item, a group or a place is read from its event alone. The trail is
  indexed by who acted and for whom.
- **Exported times end with `Z`**, as the API writes them (P-311).
- **The install guide says how roles are best given** (P-308): by groups for each team,
  the first Admin with a role of its own, one or two Admins kept so as a way back in, and
  the highest role winning.

## Fixes

- A label of 40 characters stays inside the colour picker's sample on a window of 360
  pixels (P-311).
- The pages of a service account and of an agent speak of an account that holds tokens,
  not of a person who signs in: no sessions, no access code promised on bring back.
- Browser scenarios that stalled on a busy host wait for what they read (P-309).

## Additional Notes

- **Upgrade.** Migration `0018` turns the yes or no of a service account into the kind
  of an account, adds the person an agent is answerable to and the person an event of an
  agent's was made for, and indexes the audit trail by both; nothing else that is held
  changes. Scripts that sent or read `is_service_account` now use `kind`. See
  [From 0.9.16 to 0.9.17](../install.md#from-0916-to-0917).
- **This release's test** (P-305, an ordinary release): `make check`, `make acceptance`,
  the browser scenarios on the host of every spec file changed since 0.9.16, one
  `make deb`, and of the package test `--upgrade` from 0.9.16, `--failed-upgrade`,
  `--fresh` and, for the first time, `--browser-check` in place of `--e2e`.
- **Two reviews of the server, one of the web in a browser, and two of the imports**
  found what was mended before the build: an agent brought back answerable to a retired
  person, a bulk edit and a file's change of a record taking links to retired records,
  and the warnings that left out a person's agents, among others.
- **Next, 0.9.18**: formats for what is typed or pasted (P-293). Then 1.0.0.
