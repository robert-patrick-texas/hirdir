# hirdir 0.9.14

The round of the person. Whoever signs in changes what is their own on a profile page:
their name, email, numbers and organization, the label and colour of their operator,
and their access code. A user account is retired, which is other than disabled, and
the list of users leaves the retired out until they are asked for. And the account,
the contact and the operators of one person are retired together, in one confirmation
that says what becomes of each.

## New

- **A profile of one's own** (P-289, P-294). The name of who is signed in, in the menu
  bar beside the mark of health and the role, is a link to Profile. There a person
  changes the name, email, first and second number and organization of the contact
  they are, and the label and colour of each operator they are behind, the colour by
  the picker. Every role does this for itself, View-only too, and nobody for another:
  the routes are `PATCH /auth/me/contact` and `PATCH /auth/me/operators/{hid}`, which
  name no other record. Each change is in the audit trail under the person's name. An
  account with no contact is told so, with whom to ask, and is offered its access code
  alone. Each part has a button of its own, named for what it saves.
- **A person changes their own access code** (P-295), on Profile, with the code they
  have. Every other session of theirs ends and the one in hand stays signed in. A
  wrong current code is counted as a failed sign-in is, so that a session left open is
  no way to try codes, and is in the audit trail. `POST /auth/me/access-code`.
- **A typed access code has a least length** (P-295): the setting
  `access_code_min_length`, 12 characters from the start, from 1 to 64, which an Admin
  changes on the Settings page. It holds for a code a person chooses and for one an
  Admin types, on the New user form, on a user's page and in `hirdir admin`; each form
  says the length. A code that `hirdir` makes is not held to it, and a code that is
  held already is not looked at: nobody is signed out.
- **A retired account** (P-292, P-296). A user is active, disabled or retired.
  Disabled is an account that may not sign in for now; retired is the account of
  somebody who has left, kept for the audit trail and for what it did. Retiring ends
  its sessions, erases its access code, takes it out of its groups and takes away its
  own role, each written to the audit trail. Admin, Users shows the active and the
  disabled; Show has Retired, which lists the retired by themselves, and Export CSV
  exports what the list shows. A retired account is in no picker and in no gap.
- **Bring back** (P-296, P-298), for an Admin, on the page of a retired account: it is
  active again with a new access code, shown once, and with no role and no group
  until an Admin gives them.
- **A retired account keeps its username** (P-292). A new account of that name is
  refused, with the retired one named and a link to it, where an Admin renames it to
  free the name or brings it back. The audit trail shows every event under the
  username the account has now.
- **The chain of a person** (P-291): the page of a user, of a contact and of an
  operator each shows the operators, the contact and the user of that one person,
  each a link, a retired one marked.
- **A person retired together** (P-291, P-297). Retire, on any of the three pages,
  lists each linked record with Retire ticked from the start; the Admin unticks what
  stays. For each operator there is a choice: release the label, hand it over to
  another person, leave it open, or keep it. One rule holds: a contact is not retired
  while its account or a label still stands on it. The button that confirms says what
  it will do as the ticks stand, "Release KL only" or "Release KL and retire 2 more".
  One press makes one change, each record audited as if it were done by itself. A
  request that says nothing of a linked record is refused, with the records named, and
  nothing changes. Where a linked record changes while the confirmation is open, it
  reads its list again and says so, with what was chosen kept.
- **`hirdir profile`** shows and changes one's own details, label, colour and access
  code; **`hirdir users retire`**, **`hirdir users bring-back`**,
  **`hirdir contacts retire`** and **`hirdir operators retire`** print what is linked
  and take what becomes of each, with `--dry-run`; `hirdir users list --retired`.

## Changed

- **A retired user, contact or operator is brought back by an Admin alone** (P-298).
  Until now the Inventory role brought back a contact or an operator. Retiring one is
  still the Inventory role's, and every other kind of record is brought back as
  before.
- **Retiring an operator that has somebody behind it, or a contact that is a user or
  is behind a label, says what becomes of the linked records.** A request with the
  version alone is refused with those records named. A script that retires either
  sends `linked`; see the upgrade notes.
- **A retired operator is not brought back onto a contact that is still retired.**
  The contact is brought back first, or taken off the label, and the label's page
  offers both.
- **A refusal of a retirement names each record as a person knows it**, an operator by
  its label, a contact by its name and a user by its username.
- **`GET /auth/me` answers the person's contact** with its organization and version,
  and the operators they are behind that are not retired. It listed the retired too.
- **The users' file has a `record_status` column**, after `state`.
- **A request refused as not signed in forgets only the session it was sent with**, so
  that one on its way while a person changes their access code does not sign the page
  out.
- **The Audit page has Who**, for an Admin: the list of who acted, a retired account
  still among them, marked.

## Fixes

- **`hirdir sign-in` works for an account with no role.** It kept the session and then
  failed on a setting such an account may not read.

## Additional Notes

- **Upgrade.** Migration `0017` adds the record status of a user, `active` for every
  account, and changes no record: nobody is retired by the upgrade, neither a disabled
  account nor one whose contact is retired. See
  [From 0.9.13 to 0.9.14](../install.md#from-0913-to-0914).
- **The menu bar shows the username**, as before, as the link to Profile; the name of
  the person is the link's title and on Home.
- **An account with no role**, as one that was just brought back, signs in, sees
  nothing of the inventory, and changes its own details and access code. Its Profile
  names no least length until a code is refused, and takes a colour as typed.
- **Any account can give its contact a name another contact has**, as the Inventory
  role always could. A file that names a contact by a name two contacts share is
  rejected, and names the email address or the HID as the way to say which.
- **Next, 1.0.0**: no new feature. Timing at 50,000 items, the upgrade test from every
  earlier release, accessibility, and the documentation complete. After it: the words
  the pages show gone over for plain writing (P-290), and formats for what is typed or
  pasted (P-293).
