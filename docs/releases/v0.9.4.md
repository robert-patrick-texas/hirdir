# hirdir 0.9.4

What the owner asked for after 0.9.3 was published: the rules of which group a value
comes from, written in one place and shown for every value; a minor item's own groups
before the item it is in; what an operator is on, drawn; and a leaderboard of operators,
groups, models and sites, with every report ranked.

## New

- **Why a value is what it is.** Under Values in force, the page of an item shows the walk
  of each value it has from elsewhere as a row of boxes joined by arrows: its own, its own
  groups ranked a stack before a cluster before a controller domain before a custom group,
  the item it is in and that item's groups, and for the operator the location, the site and
  each region. The box that decides is marked, a value passed over is struck through, a
  step with nothing is dashed, a retired operator or group says so, and a value of the
  item's own says what it replaces. The values of its model are shown the same way, with
  the default and the minimum of a support weight. A group's page shows its own walk up the
  groups it is nested in. `GET /items/{hid}/resolution` and
  `GET /device-groups/{hid}/resolution` answer the same.
- **What an operator is on.** An operator's page draws a tree: the operator with what it is
  in force for, then the places given it, the groups given it and its own items, each box
  with how many items have it from there, the places or groups beneath that have it too,
  and the items there that are someone else's. Every count opens the item list at exactly
  those items. `GET /operators/{hid}/assignments` answers it, and the item list filters by
  how an item has its operator, `effective.operator_hid.source`, and from which record,
  `effective.operator_hid.source_hid`.
- **A leaderboard.** Every table of the Summary, the Management report and the Scoreboard
  has a rank and a bar beside each count, and the Summary and the Management report open
  most first. The Scoreboard ranks the operators, with the health of what each looks after
  in one column; beneath them, how each operator has its items, its own, from the item they
  are in, from a group or from a place, and the groups and places given it; and the device
  groups, each with its members, the groups nested in it and everything under it.
- **By group and by model.** The Summary and the Management report count by device group,
  each row holding its members, those of the groups nested in it and their minor items, and
  by model, the models of every vendor ranked in one table, `Cisco C9300-48P` beside
  `Juniper EX4400-48P`. The Catalog lists how many items are of each model. The item list
  finds what is under a group with `group:within:<group>`, and what is under none with
  `group:empty:true`.
- **The rules, written down.** "Groups, and which of them decides", on the data model page,
  says what may be in more than one group, how an item's groups are ranked, what breaks a
  tie, and what happens when two groups both have a value, for the teams that run hirdir
  or build on it.

## Fixes

- **A minor item's own groups come first.** An optic in a switch that is in a group of its
  own has that group's operator, location, planned location and owner, where until now only
  the groups of the switch counted. The item list, the reports and a file all walk the same
  way, so a file that names what an item has anyway leaves it inheriting.
- **A retired group passes nothing down** to the groups nested in it, as a retired group
  counts for nothing.
- **A file names a row's location before its operator**, so a row that names the operator
  of its new place leaves the item inheriting it, as a row that names what an item has
  anyway does.

## Additional Notes

- Upgrading from 0.9.3 changes nothing that is held: there is no migration, and the schema
  stays at revision `0012`. A minor item that is a member of a group of its own may show
  another value in force after the upgrade, worked out when it is read. See "From 0.9.3 to
  0.9.4" in [Install and operate](../install.md#from-093-to-094).
- Going back to 0.9.3 needs no backup: it is the one `apt install --allow-downgrades`
  command.
- P-175 to P-181 are this release's, each in its row of [Decisions](../decisions.md), and
  P-179 sets out the views of the data to come after 1.0.0: a pivot, treemaps, heatmaps,
  bar and bubble charts, flows and drawings, each another view of a list, drawn in the
  colours of the theme with its numbers beside it. They add no scenario, as a release of
  corrections adds none.
- Two reviews of the server found that walking each level at first made filtering by a
  value in force several times slower; the walk now ranks an item's groups once, and on
  50,000 items the filters, the summaries and the reports take about what they took in
  0.9.3. The page of what an operator is on takes from 2 to 6 seconds there, and goes to
  the timing of 1.0.0.
- Every component is at its latest production release. Nothing is held back.
- Scenarios S01 to S35 and S39 to S53 pass.
