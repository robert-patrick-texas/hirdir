# hirdir 0.9.3

What the owner asked for after 0.9.2 was published: every person is a contact, of which
a user and an operator are each a part; a username that an Admin changes; the kinds of
external system a list that a site changes; and the form that changes a setting opened
where the setting is listed.

## New

- **Every person is a contact.** The contacts are the one list of people, each with a
  name, an organization, an email, a phone and now a second phone, and notes. A user, who
  signs in, and an operator, who looks after what a label is on, are each a part that a
  contact plays, and hold only what is theirs: a user its username, access code, groups
  and roles; an operator its label, colour and description. Nothing is copied from the
  contact to the part, so a person who signs in, looks after a set of devices and is the
  technical contact of a site is one contact, and a new number entered once shows in all
  three places. A user shows its contact's name, email and two numbers, and its page
  changes them on the contact; a new user is made with a contact there is, a new one, or
  none yet, and a service account has none. A contact says which user it is.
  `hirdir admin create-user` takes `--name`, `--email`, `--phone` and `--phone2` to make
  the contact of the first Admin.
- **Internal and external operators.** An operator is internal where the person behind
  it is a user, and external, to hirdir, where not; a label with nobody behind it, or
  whose person is retired, is open and is neither. Operators are listed with it and with
  the person's email and two numbers, which are changed there, on the contact. The person
  behind a label is chosen from the contacts that are users, the other contacts, or a new
  person. The list of operators filters and sorts by it, and the item list by the scope
  of the operator in force.
- **A username is changed.** An Admin gives a user another username on the user's page.
  The user keeps its sessions, its tokens, its groups and its roles, and signs in under
  the new name from then on; the trail names whoever made a change by the name they have
  now, and keeps the old and the new in the event of the rename.
- **The kinds of external system are a list.** `system_kind` is in Admin › Choices, as
  "External system kind", loaded with the eight kinds that were fixed, which a site
  renames, adds to and makes inactive. A system holding a kind made inactive keeps it. A
  system made with no kind is given `other` while that is offered, and is refused where
  it is not.

## Fixes

- **A form opens beneath its row.** On Admin › Settings, Change opened its form below
  every setting, so an Admin scrolled down to set it and back up again: the first
  setting's form was 3,604 pixels below its button. It now opens directly beneath the
  row of the setting, 174 pixels away, with the focus in it and back on Change when it
  closes. Tags, Lifecycle rules, Files and the Files and links of a record do the same.
- **The Settings table fits.** At a window 1,280 pixels wide the table was 1,453 pixels
  in a column of 1,140, and its Change buttons were half out of view. Long names wrap and
  the two buttons of a row stand one above the other, so it fits; a narrower window
  scrolls it sideways as before.

## Additional Notes

- **This upgrade changes what is held**, which a release before 1.0.0 may do. Each user
  is given a contact from its display name and email: the contact that already has that
  email, which is left as it is; for a user with no email, or one that is no address, the
  one contact of its name; or a new one. Users that may sign in go first, so of two of one email the one in
  use has it. Then the users' display names and emails are dropped, a service account's
  with them; each user's audit event keeps what was dropped. Contacts gain a second
  phone, blank, and the schema goes from revision `0011` to `0012`. See "From 0.9.2 to
  0.9.3" in [Install and operate](../install.md#from-092-to-093).
- Going back to 0.9.2 is by the backup the upgrade took: what was entered since is lost.
- For a script that reads the API: `POST /users` and `PATCH /users/{hid}` no longer take
  `display_name` or `email`, which a user answers from its contact; a user is given its
  contact with `contact_hid`, or a new one with `contact`; `PATCH /users/{hid}` takes
  `username`; and `external_systems.kind` is any value of `system_kind`.
- `hirdir admin reset` keeps the contact each user is, with its history, as it keeps the
  accounts.
- P-170 to P-174 are this release's, each in its row of [Decisions](../decisions.md).
  P-174 is what two reviews of the server found and how it was put right. They add no
  scenario, as a release of corrections adds none.
- Every component is at its latest production release. Nothing is held back.
- Scenarios S01 to S35 and S39 to S53 pass.
