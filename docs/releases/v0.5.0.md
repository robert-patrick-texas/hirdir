# hirdir 0.5.0

Places and people. Regions hold sites and sites hold locations, an item is given a
location, and contacts and shipping details are set once on a region or a site and
reach every place beneath it. An owner has its people, and what is with an
owner outside the team is marked as an exception.

## New

- **Regions, sites and locations.** A region may be within another, up to sixteen deep; a
  site is in one region; a location is in one site and may be within another
  location: a building, a floor, a room, a rack, up to eight deep. Each has a name, an
  optional code, tags, and may be a draft. A site and a location have a street
  address and coordinates. Each has a path, the names from the top region down:
  `Americas > Central > Site-2 > Building 1 > Room 102`. The path is worked out when
  it is read and stored nowhere.
- **Where an item is.** An item is given a location, and its site and its regions
  follow. A minor item is where the item above it is, and says so, unless it is given
  a place of its own. A device group may be given a location too. On the item list
  `location:within:Americas > Central` finds everything at or beneath a region, a site
  or a location, by its path or its HID, and `site` and `region` filter and sort by
  name.
- **The tree of places.** `GET /geography/tree`, the Geography page and `hirdir
  geography tree` show the regions, their sites and the locations within them, each
  with the items and the components at or beneath it, for the whole inventory or for
  any view of it. The items that are nowhere are counted beside the tree, so the
  numbers add up.
- **Moving a site.** Giving a site another region, or a region another parent, is one
  change of one record and one audit event. The paths of everything in it, the
  contacts it inherits and the totals of both regions follow at once, and no item is
  written to.
- **Contacts.** A contact is a person or a shared mailbox, entered once. It is
  assigned to a region, a site or a location in a role: shipping, technical, federal,
  operational or escalation, a list that can be edited. A place with no contact of
  its own for a role has those of the nearest place above it, and every answer says
  which level they came from. A place can also say "none here, and do not look
  further up".
- **Shipping address and instructions.** A region, a site and a location may each
  hold a shipping address and shipping instructions. A place with none has the
  address of the nearest place above it that has one, taken whole and never field by
  field, and the instructions likewise by themselves. So the address is entered once,
  on the region, for the sites that share it. A site or a location whose deliveries
  go to its own street address says so with one tick, "Deliveries go to the street
  address", and nothing is typed a second time. The one site with another destination
  has it entered, and that is shown as an override of the region's.
- **Inherited or overridden, at a glance.** What a place has from a level above is
  shown lighter, with the level it came from, on its page and in its form. What it
  has of its own where a level above has one too is marked as an override, and says
  of what.
- **The operator.** An item and a device group may be given an operator: the one
  person of the team who looks after it, apart from its owner. A group is given its
  operator once and its members have it, a stack's switches with their power
  supplies, the access points of a controller domain; a group with none has that of
  the group it is nested in; and the one switch that somebody else looks after is
  given its own. Every answer says where the operator came from. What nobody is
  assigned to is the blank row of a report by operator, a number on the first page,
  and one filter away: `effective.operator_hid:empty:true`. A bulk edit assigns many
  items at once.
- **The people of an owner.** An owner has a primary and any number of alternates,
  each a contact. Making another member the primary makes the one that had the role an
  alternate, in the same change.
- **What is with an owner outside the team is marked as an exception.** The reason
  is the owner's, said once where the owner is defined. An item or a device group
  that has such an owner is marked by that alone, shows the owner's reason, and is
  asked for nothing of its own; the minor items of such an item are marked too.
  `owner_exception:eq:true` finds them.
- **Reports by site and by region.** The summary and the management report take
  `site` and `region`. A region's row holds everything at or beneath it.
- **In files.** Regions, sites, locations, contacts, their assignments and the people
  of owners are imported and exported as CSV and JSON. An item's file says where the
  item is in the `location` column, by path. The rows of one file may stand in any order: a region
  before or after the regions within it, a room before its building, an alternate
  before the primary. The examples have a geography of three regions and three sites,
  contacts and the people of each owner, in fifteen files.
- **In the web UI.** Geography is a new page: the tree of places with what is in
  each, and for a place its path, what it has in force with the level each thing came
  from, the contacts assigned to it, and the forms to change, move and retire it.
  Contacts is a new page too. An owner's page shows its people. The item grid has
  Location, Site and Region columns in the column chooser, an item's form takes a
  location found by searching, an item and a group with an owner outside the team
  are marked with that owner's reason, and an item or a group whose location was retired after it is
  brought back in another one.
- **On the command line.** `hirdir geography tree`; `hirdir regions`, `sites`,
  `locations` and `contacts` with `list`; `hirdir locations show`, and the same for a
  site and a region, with the contacts and the shipping address in force and where
  each came from; `hirdir sites move` and `hirdir regions move`.

## Changed

- **The management report is by site** when it is asked for by nothing: the answer
  has one row for each site beside its totals, and `by` says `site` where it was
  `null`. Until items are given locations there is one row, of the items that are
  nowhere. The totals are as they were.
- **The load order of files.** Regions, sites, locations, contacts and contact
  assignments come after the catalog entries and before the owners, and the people of
  owners after the owners. See [Examples](../../examples/README.md).
- **Owners, and the records of this release, take tags.**
- **The record of an owner** lists its `tags` and its `members`, and **the record of
  an item and of a device group** the `location` it is at.

## Fixes

- **A small import or bulk edit is no longer refused as busy for no reason.** A
  preview of up to a hundred rows could be answered `503` at once, with no waiting,
  when another change was stored at the same moment. It now waits its turn like any
  other change.

## Additional Notes

- **Upgrading from 0.4.2** adds six empty tables and three columns, and changes
  nothing that is held: see
  [From 0.4.2 to 0.5.0](../install.md#from-042-to-050). Every item is nowhere until
  it is given a location. An item or a group that was already with an owner outside
  the team is marked as an exception, with the reason its owner has.
- **Some choices of this release wait for a yes or a correction** in
  [Decisions](../decisions.md), under Proposed.
- **For those who build `hirdir`:** the package test reads the pair of releases it
  upgrades between from the two packages, so a release with no migration needs no
  change to it and one with a migration needs one small file; and the browser
  scenarios run side by side, each file with an account and names of its own. See
  "Testing" in [Architecture](../architecture.md#testing).
