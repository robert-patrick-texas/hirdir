# hirdir 0.9.7

What the first days of use asked for. A page that cannot be drawn says so, and a page
older than the server says that a new version is installed; every value of a list that
names another record opens it; rows are ticked by a box, and the rows ticked are retired
at once; a past import lists the records it made, which are retired, or deleted by an
Admin where they were a mistake; a place is named by its codes; and the coordinates of a
place open a map. Nothing that is held changes: there is no migration. The API gains
five routes and several members, listed under Additional Notes.

## New

- **A page that cannot be drawn says so.** It says This page could not be drawn, shows
  the text of the fault with a button that copies it, and offers Load again and The
  first page; the menu bar stays, and going to another page clears it. A part of the
  page that cannot be fetched says the same. Before its script runs the page holds
  words as well, the product's name and Loading, and after six seconds that the page
  should be loaded again, so that a script which did not arrive leaves words and not an
  empty window. The fault is written to the browser's console and sent nowhere. Measured
  on the published 0.9.6 before anything was built: an answer of another shape than a
  page expects left 7 to 21 of 38 pages with nothing in the window on coming back to
  their tab, and the page's own script not arriving left a dark window with no words;
  on 0.9.7 the same faults leave no page empty.
- **A page older than the server says that a new version is installed.** The page knows
  the version it was built as, and asks the server what is running when its person
  comes back to its tab, at a change of page when it last asked more than five minutes
  before, and when a page could not be drawn. Where the two differ, a notice stands
  under the menu bar with a button, Load again, until the page is loaded again. The
  page never loads again by itself, so nothing typed into a form is lost, and nobody
  who is not signed in is told the version.
- **Every value of a list that names another record is a link.** In the list of items
  the stack and the groups of an item, its location, its planned location, its site and
  its region, its owner, its operator, its vendor and its model, the item it is in, its
  contracts, the systems it is registered with and the request of its purchase line
  each open that record; and likewise the masters, the owner, the places and the group
  of a device group, the vendor of a contract, an agreement, a catalog entry and a
  purchase line, the contact of an operator and of an owner, the item and the group of
  an address, and the values of the tables on pages. Such a link is in the colour of
  the text and has no underline until the pointer is on it or it has the keyboard's
  focus; the name that opens a row's own record stays the link it was. A press on a
  link selects nothing; Ctrl, or the middle button, opens it in a new tab; Enter on a
  cell opens the record it names. What is copied and exported is unchanged.
- **Rows are ticked.** Every list whose rows are selected has a box at the left of each
  row, and one in its heading, which ticks the rows read so far, says how many, and
  shows the half mark unless every row of the list is read and ticked. Copy, Export,
  Bulk edit, Retire and Delete say how many rows they will take, as "Copy (3)".
  Pressing a row ticks it or unticks it, and unticks nothing else; Shift with a press,
  or with Space, ticks the rows from the one last pressed to this one. The column of
  boxes is 24 pixels wide, and the columns of the item list that stood in a window of
  1280 pixels still do.
- **The rows ticked are retired at once.** The lists of items and of device groups have
  a button, Retire, for the Inventory role: it asks first and says how many, retires
  each as its page does, with an audit event each, and says what was retired, what was
  retired already and what was refused. It takes particular rows, at most 1,000, and is
  not offered for all that match a filter.
- **An Admin deletes an item or a device group that was a mistake.** From the rows
  ticked in a list, and from the record's page. The question shows first what the
  server would do: how many will be deleted, and which are refused, each with what
  depends on it as links. A reason must be typed. With an item go its addresses, its
  tags, its memberships of groups and its registrations; it is refused while a contract
  covers or has covered it, it arrived on a purchase line, an item is installed in it,
  it replaced or was replaced by another, a circuit is connected to it, or a reference
  file is attached to it. With a device group go its memberships, its addresses, its
  tags and its registrations; it is refused while a group is nested in it or a circuit
  is connected to it. A switch ticked with the power supplies in it goes with them.
  The audit trail keeps every field of the record as it was, what went with it, who,
  when and why, beside every event the record had before; a deletion removes nothing
  from it. The page of a record that was deleted says so, with when, by whom and the
  reason. Every other kind of record is retired, as before.
- **A past import lists the records it made.** On Import, an import that was applied
  shows What it made: the row of the file, the kind of record, its name as a link, and
  what it is now, active, a draft, retired or deleted; then the vendors and the catalog
  entries it made only because a row named them. For an import of items or of device
  groups the rows are ticked, and Retire and Delete are there for the roles that have
  them. `hirdir imports created` prints the same.
- **A place is named by its codes.** A step of a place's path is its name, or else its
  code; a path may begin at the code of a region or a site; and after a site, the code
  of a location finds it at whatever depth: `S2 > R102` finds what
  `Americas > Central > Site-2 > Building 1 > Room 102` finds. That holds in a file, in
  a filter and in a command. An export, and a copy, write places the short way where
  Places by code is ticked, and what they write loads back to the same places.
- **The coordinates of a place open a map.** The latitude and longitude of a site and
  of a location are a link, in a new tab, to the address that the setting `map_link`
  names, Google Maps unless an Admin changes it. Blank, the coordinates are plain
  numbers, as for a network with no way out. Following the link sends the coordinates
  of that place to the map's site, and nothing else.
- **Fixing a wrong import**, a section of [Interfaces](../interfaces.md#fixing-a-wrong-import),
  says what to do in each case, each step tried: the preview's counts before applying;
  wrong values on the right records, corrected by exporting the rows with their HIDs
  and importing them back, or by a bulk edit, which gives many items another model at
  once; the kind of a model, a switch or a router, changed once on its catalog entry
  for every item of it; and records that should never have been, retired or deleted
  from the import's list.

## Changed

- **Pressing a row of a list ticks it, and no longer unticks the others.** With boxes on
  show, one stray press cleared every tick; and since most cells of a row are links,
  Ctrl with a press opens a link in a new tab, as a browser does, where it added the
  row to the selection. Shift with a press takes the rows between and unticks nothing;
  where some of them have not been read yet, in a list longer than a hundred rows, they
  cannot be ticked, and the list says so and how many.
- **The buttons of a list say how many rows they take.** "Copy" is "Copy (3)" with
  three rows ticked, and "Bulk edit (all 1,204)" for all that match.
- **The commands that retire and delete take several.** `hirdir items retire` and
  `hirdir groups retire` take any number of items or groups, up to 1,000, and
  `hirdir items delete` and `hirdir groups delete` are new: they print what would be
  deleted and what refused, and ask, unless `--yes`.
- **Each file of `hirdir admin load-examples` is loaded under a request of its own**, so
  that each import of the examples lists what it made, and not what the others did.
- **A value of a setting that is one long word breaks where its column needs it to.**
  The address of a map, unbroken, made its row of Admin, Settings 464 pixels tall; it
  is 195.

## Fixes

- None of an earlier release: what the two reviews of this release found was mended
  before it, and is under Additional Notes.

## Additional Notes

- Upgrading from 0.9.6 changes nothing that is held: there is no migration, and the
  schema stays at revision `0012`. See "From 0.9.6 to 0.9.7" in
  [Install and operate](../install.md#from-096-to-097).
- Going back to 0.9.6 needs no backup: it is the one `apt install --allow-downgrades`
  command.
- **The blank window that began this was not reproduced.** An operator's page in
  Microsoft Edge was found blank and dark on coming back to its tab. In a headless
  Chromium on the build host, a tab resting on each of 43 pages of a published release
  with the server upgraded underneath, for four pairs of releases, never emptied, since
  the page fetches its other pages ahead; a session that had ended brought the sign-in
  page; the error page of a proxy, and no network at all, left every page standing. The
  two ways to an empty window that were found are closed by this release. If the fault
  page of 0.9.7 is ever seen, its text says what happened; if a window is ever empty
  all the same, the cause is outside what the page can catch, and may be the browser's
  own handling of a tab it put to sleep.
- **The API gains**, with nothing taken away: `POST /items/retire` and
  `POST /device-groups/retire`; `POST /items/delete` and `POST /device-groups/delete`,
  with `dry_run`; `GET /imports/{hid}/created`; `map_url` on a site and a location, and
  the setting `map_link`; `place_codes` on an export; `catalog_entry_hid` on an item
  named by another record, and `vendor_hid` on a contract named so; and `deleted`, with
  when, by whom and why, in the `404` of an item or a device group that was deleted.
- **Only items and device groups are deleted** (P-227). Deleting the other kinds of
  record, under the same rule, is on the list of what follows 1.0.0. The vendors and
  catalog entries a wrong import made in passing are listed with it and are retired on
  their pages.
- **Typing into the cells of a list**, a live edit, stays with the cell ranges of the
  grid on the list of what follows 1.0.0 (P-233), and so does choosing a place from a
  map (P-229).
- P-227 to P-236 are this release's, each in its row of [Decisions](../decisions.md).
  Two reviewers who had not built the work read it. The one of the server found no
  fault in deleting: after a deletion no row of any table still named the record but
  the audit trail and the import that made it, nothing was removed that was not the
  record's alone, and requests made at the same moment left nothing behind. What the
  two found was mended before the release, and a third reviewer then read the mending
  itself. Among what they found: with the boxes on show one stray press on a row
  cleared every tick, and then, in the first mending of that, Shift with a press after
  an untick dropped every row outside its range; the box in a list's heading read as
  all ticked with a hundred of 260 rows; an import listed the vendors and models that
  another had made, wherever two shared a request, as all those of the loaded examples
  did; a fault page stayed when its own page was opened again from the menu; and an
  address of a map with a bracket in its host answered a server error and, stored,
  would have stopped every list of places.
- **Answered by the owner while this release was tested, and built in 0.9.8** (P-237 to
  P-240): a code that a region has is refused for a site, and the reverse, where today
  a path that begins with such a code finds neither; an Admin removes a replacement
  that was a mistake, where today an item that is part of one can never be deleted; the
  username in the list of contacts is a link for an Admin; and the tables of the
  scoreboard, the totals, the settings and the audit trail, looked through by a script
  for values that should be links, are gone through one by one.
- Left for a later release, each seen: a file chosen for a place in the moment before
  the page has read the kinds of file is not given its first kind, a photograph for a
  picture, and the person chooses it, which is older than this release and failed one
  scenario once on a busy host; the box in a heading ticks the rows read so far, a hundred at a time, so a wrong
  import of several hundred items is retired from the import's own list, a page at a
  time, or after scrolling through the list; a fault outside drawing, in a press or a
  request, is written to the console and shown nowhere; and everything in a browser
  was tried in Chromium, not in Edge or Firefox. From 0.9.6 there remain Replace in one
  press and a stack made of a switch that stands alone (O-02), which with the things
  that run on another, a virtual device context, a virtual machine and a container
  (P-226), are for 0.9.8.
- `npm audit` reports nothing: the advisory against `picomatch` that 0.9.6 named is gone
  with the releases of this week.
- Thirty-five browser scenarios are new, in six files: 198 in all. They add no
  numbered scenario, as a release of corrections adds none.
- The package test carries one `SKIP`: with no migration there is none to make fail, so
  that check is skipped and says so. The pass upgrades and goes back all the same.
- Every component is at its latest production release. Nothing is held back.
- Scenarios S01 to S35 and S39 to S53 pass.
