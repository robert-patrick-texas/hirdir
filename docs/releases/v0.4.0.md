# hirdir 0.4.0

Structure. An item may be in another item, switches are grouped into stacks and
controllers into clusters and domains, items and groups have addresses, and the
inventory is counted: components beside managed nodes, with the support weight that a
stack saves.

## New

- **Minor items.** A power supply, a module or a line card is an item that is in
  another item, with a position such as "PS-A" or "Slot 3". It has the owner and the
  lifecycle state of the item it is in, and says so, unless it is given its own: the
  power supply that failed is moved to Pending-Removal by itself, and returned to
  following its switch afterwards. Moving the switch changes what its minor items show
  and writes to none of them. Minor items are not counted, and nest up to three deep.
- **Device groups.** A stack, a cluster, a controller domain, or a group of another
  kind: a named set of items, each with a role and the dates it joined and left. A
  stack has one master and shows the master's serial number; making another member the
  master changes it at once. A controller domain holds controllers, or a cluster of
  them, and the access points or phones they manage, and each of those lists its
  controllers. Groups nest, carry tags and an owner, and are retired, not deleted.
- **Replacing an item.** For an RMA, a refresh or a project, the item that leaves and
  the item that takes its place are two records linked by a replacement, with the
  reason and a reference. The new item takes over the old one's place in a stack or any
  other group, so the stack stays the same record with the same history.
- **Networks and addresses.** An item or a group has any number of IPv4 and IPv6
  addresses, one of them its primary management address. An address may be under a
  network label, a VRF or a tenant, and the same address under two labels is two
  addresses. The same address twice under one label is accepted, because shared and
  virtual addresses are real, and is listed in the duplicates report.
- **The summary.** Any view of the items is counted: the components, the managed nodes,
  where a stack or a cluster is one, the support weight, with the stack discount of
  each model, the estimated value and the annual support cost, which no discount
  touches. It is given whole and by vendor, kind, type, function, owner, lifecycle
  state, disposition or tag, on the first page, under Reports, from `GET
  /reports/summary` and from `hirdir report summary`.
- **In the web UI.** The item page shows where an item is, what is in it, its groups,
  controllers and addresses, and what it replaced. Groups, Addresses and Reports are
  new pages. The item grid shows a minor item under the state and the owner it has
  from the item above, lighter, and finds it by them.
- **On the command line.** `hirdir groups list` and `show`, `hirdir networks list`,
  `hirdir addresses list`, `hirdir report summary` and `hirdir report duplicates`.
  `hirdir items show` says where an item is and what it has from the item above.
- **In files.** Networks, addresses, device groups and their members are imported and
  exported as CSV and JSON. An item's file names the item it is in, in the `parent`
  column, by serial number, asset tag or name. The examples hold two stacks, a cluster
  in a controller domain, minor items and addresses, in nine files.

## Changed

- **The load order of files.** Networks come before items, and device groups, their
  members and addresses after them. See [Examples](../../examples/README.md).
- **Retiring an item ends its memberships of groups.** The master of a stack is not
  retired while the stack has other members: another is made master first.
- **A bulk edit and an import work an item before its minor items**, wherever their
  rows stand, so a row that holds the owner or the state its item has anyway from the
  item above leaves it inheriting.
- **The item grid's State, Disposition, Expected online and Owner columns** show,
  filter and sort by the value in force. For an item that is in no other item nothing
  changes. The item's own state is still a field of the filter box, as
  `lifecycle_state`.
- **An effective value may be true or false.** In the API, `effective` now also holds
  `owner_hid`, `lifecycle_state`, `disposition` and `expected_online`, and the last is
  a truth value where the others are text.

## Additional Notes

- Every component is at its latest production release. Nothing is held back.
- Upgrading from 0.3.0 takes the schema from revision `0004` to `0005`: five tables are
  created and the items gain two columns. No item is changed: each is a major item, as
  before. See "From 0.3.0 to 0.4.0" in [Install and operate](../install.md#upgrade).
- Places arrive in 0.5.0. Until then a minor item inherits its owner and its standing,
  and not yet a location, and scenario S18 is tried without its location half.
- A replacement moves neither item in the lifecycle: that is a move of its own, with
  its own reason. Replacements in files and in reports come with 0.6.0.
- A stack with no members yet is accepted, so that a file of groups can be loaded
  before the file of their members. From its first member on, an active stack has at
  least two members and one master; members are added several at a time, or the stack
  is built as a draft. In a file of members a stack is checked once the whole file is
  worked, and the rows of a stack it would leave wrong are rejected together.
- A draft stack is not yet one device on the network: its members count as the items
  they are, and earn no discount, unless the view includes drafts.
- An address, and a network that no address is under, is removed outright. The audit
  trail holds what it was.
- `mgmt_address` and `mgmt_network` in a file of items or of device groups set the
  primary management address. An export writes them only when they are asked for,
  because every address is in the file of addresses.
- Two requests at the same moment cannot put one item into two stacks, or two items
  inside each other: a change of that kind waits for the one before it.
- `make package-test` carries an inventory across the upgrade from 0.3.0 and compares
  every item field for field, loads the nine example files through nginx, and makes a
  stack, a replacement and duplicate addresses under the prefix.
- The text of a release on GitHub is now given with each paragraph on one line, by
  `make release-body`, so that it is shown at the full width of the page. See
  [Release process](../release-process.md#the-text-of-the-github-release).
- The choices this release made where the documentation was silent are proposals P-60
  to P-77 in [Decisions](../decisions.md). Each is built, and none is answered yet.
- Scenarios S18 to S23 of the [build plan](../build-plan.md) pass, with S01 to S17.
