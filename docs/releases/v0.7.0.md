# hirdir 0.7.0

Renewals in practice, and links. A worklist and an alert after sign-in say what has to
be acted on to keep the cover going; an agreement is renewed with its member contracts,
each confirmed by the operator; a model or a device is flagged as not to be renewed;
imports are applied in the background; the capacity balance is kept week by week; and
regions, sites, locations and models take links.

## New

- **The renewal worklist.** One page, one report (`GET /reports/renewals`) and one
  command (`hirdir renewals`) for the contracts that end within the warning period, those
  that have lapsed, those whose renewal is pending and for how long, marked when overdue,
  those the team marked not to renew, the members to review after an agreement was
  renewed, and the items more than one contract covers, each under its vendor and its
  agreement. Each count leads to its rows.
- **A renewal alert after sign-in.** A person with the Inventory or the Admin role is
  shown, once a day, how many contracts and agreements have lapsed, have a renewal
  pending or end within the warning period, each leading to the worklist.
- **An agreement renewed with its members.** The renewal lists every contract that
  shares the agreement's period, a contract in it with no end of its own: one, some or
  all of them are selected, and each keeps its number, takes a new number that begins
  with the agreement's new term, or ends with this term, with the status the operator
  confirms for it. No member's status changes by itself: those still pending or expired
  are listed to review, in the renewal's answer, on the agreement and on the worklist.
- **A contract's history in parts.** The earlier terms of a contract are the parts of
  its history, each with its days and what began it: being made, a renewal, joining an
  agreement or leaving one, each with the period it had in force then.
- **Not to be renewed.** A model, or one device, is flagged as one whose support is not
  bought again, such as an access point or a telephone whose cover came with its
  purchase. A renewal leaves it behind, its lack of cover is no gap, and the coverage
  report counts what is left out on purpose by model. A contract may carry the team's
  own note that it is not to be renewed, which changes nothing by itself.
- **The contacts of agreements and contracts.** Account managers and support contacts
  are contact records, any number of each, and a contract has those of its agreement
  where it names none of its own, with the source shown. The free text stays.
- **Imports in the background.** Applying a file or a bulk edit answers at once and is
  worked 50 rows to a batch beside other work, its progress shown. A row whose record
  changed after the preview is not applied and is listed; one that stops partway is
  resumed, and nothing is applied twice.
- **Purchase requests as procurement has them.** A line keeps its part number and
  description; a line with no model is not tracked and does not hold its request open;
  a file of lines matches each part number to the catalog, the exact one first, and
  makes no catalog entry; a line's model is corrected at any time, with the items that
  arrived on it following or staying.
- **Settings rather than constants.** Five values that a site may want otherwise are
  settings with their defaults: the renewal warning period, when a pending renewal is
  overdue, how far ahead the end-of-life report looks and which date it is of, and how
  many items one request may cover. The settings page lists every fixed value beside
  them, with why it is fixed. `hirdir admin config` writes a complete configuration
  file, every key with its default and its description.
- **Capacity over time.** The balance is recorded each Monday by the server and again
  by hand, a week's figures may be corrected with a note, and the trend is a graph of
  one point a week. A line multiplies the staff lines it names. The report stays one
  balance for the whole team, with the demand of each region beside it.
- **Links.** A region, a site, a location and a catalog entry take links, each an
  address and its text; an item shows those of its model, and files carry them.

## Changed

- **The coverage report is counted in one pass and kept** until a change or the next
  day, and the summary of the alert is worked out just after midnight and when the
  server starts. A filtered report counts the lapsed contracts of its view.
- **An apply answers `202`.** A script that loads files in order waits for each job to
  be `applied` before the next: see "From 0.6.1 to 0.7.0" in
  [Install and operate](../install.md#from-061-to-070). Scenario S15 reads accordingly:
  the row changed since the preview is reported, and the others are applied.
- **One server to a state directory.** `hirdir admin serve` holds a lock, and a second
  one on the same directory is refused.

## Additional Notes

- Every component is at its latest production release. Nothing is held back.
- **Upgrading from 0.6.1 runs migration `0009`**, which adds the new fields and tables
  and changes nothing that is held but the counts of imports applied before, which are
  given what an apply now keeps. The way back is the backup, as
  [Install and operate](../install.md#from-061-to-070) says.
- P-115 to P-138 are the particulars of this release, each built as it stands with the
  owner's answers in its row of [Decisions](../decisions.md). P-139, a support weight of
  a place, is planned for 0.8.0.
- **For those who build `hirdir`:** the server was built in parts by several agents at
  once from one schema, reviewed twice by readers who had not written it, the second
  time after the answers were built, and the web UI was built in two lanes, each running
  its own browser scenarios.
- The overview opens with a picture of `hirdir` as the source of truth for the fleet,
  and the lifecycle whiteboard is redrawn sharper, black on white, at the top of the
  [Data model](../data-model.md).
- Scenarios S01 to S35 and S39 to S46 pass.
- **Timing**, on the 50,000 items of `make fixture`, by the method of D-165. A kept
  answer of the coverage report, the worklist or the alert takes 10 to 34 ms; the
  worklist and the alert worked out anew take about 200 ms; the grid's first page and
  the list with no filter meet the target, as in 0.6.1. Still left to the timing of
  1.0.0, with their numbers in "Timing at scale" in
  [Architecture](../architecture.md#timing-at-scale): the coverage filters of the grid
  (376 to 652 ms), the coverage report worked out anew (1.4 s, counted section by
  section after one pass over the items was measured three times slower), the capacity
  balance (1.3 s), and **an import of 10,000 rows, which misses both of its qualities**:
  122 s to preview against 30 s, and 168 s to apply against 60 s. The apply no longer
  holds up other people's work while it runs: a sign-in beside it waited 0.9 s at the
  longest.
