# hirdir 0.8.0

Circuits. The telecommunication circuits the team looks after are kept beside the
inventory and share what it holds: their ends are locations with their site contacts,
their carriers are vendors whose account team and support desk are named once, and
their devices are items and groups. What they cost is reported by year, by carrier, by
site and by group, a part month as the carrier bills it. Places may weigh by themselves,
a default and a minimum support weight give a quick estimate of the capacity balance,
and Try answers what other figures would give without saving anything.

## New

- **Circuits.** A circuit has a name, the enterprise's own circuit ID, a type and a
  speed (its port, and the rates it is limited to down and up, shown short as
  `1G / 200M / 20M`), an A side and a Z side, each a location or, for an end in the
  carrier's network, a note of where it ends; its primary carrier and any number of
  others, each with its part and the number it gives the circuit; its billing account
  and contract; its install, activation and disconnection dates; whether it carries
  production; its support weight; and its owner, the person or team responsible for
  it. Who pays a charge-back is a tag or a note. The devices it connects are items or
  device groups, each on its side, and an item, a group and a location show their
  circuits; a replacement hands a router's circuits to the one that took its place.
- **The contacts of carriers.** A vendor names its account team and its support desk
  once, as contact records, and each of its circuits has them, with their source,
  unless it names its own. Each side has the site contacts of its location, resolved as
  the contacts of a place are, with the level each came from.
- **What circuits cost.** A circuit's cost is the lines the carrier bills each month,
  with their dates, a credit below zero, and its annual cost twelve times their sum.
  `GET /reports/circuit-costs` and `hirdir report circuit-costs` give the cost of a
  fiscal year by carrier, site, region, group, type, owner or tag, with the monthly
  figure beside it: a month a circuit is in service for only in part counts for its
  days, as the carrier bills it. A circuit counts at its A side's site unless it is
  switched to its Z side; a setting gives new circuits their side. Each row leads to its
  circuits.
- **Groups of circuits**, such as Internet access or a cloud on-ramp: a circuit may be
  in several, and a group's cost is one sum.
- **Circuits in files, lists, the command line and the web UI**: seven new kinds of
  file, a grid of circuits filtered, sorted and exported by the server, a page for each
  circuit and group, and `hirdir circuits` and `hirdir circuit-groups`.
- **The support weight of a place.** A region, a site or a location may weigh by
  itself, for what makes the same devices harder to support there: distance, a
  language, a campus spread across a city, a restricted site. It is demand in the
  capacity report, and not passed down.
- **A default and a minimum support weight** for major items, sites and circuits, each
  blank until an Admin sets it: the default is counted where nothing gives a weight, with
  the source `setting`, and a weight below the minimum is raised to it, which the record
  says. The stack discount is applied after each member's weight is resolved.
- **Try**, a what-if of the capacity report: other defaults and minimums, another weight
  one engineer carries, another stack discount, and staff lines added, changed or left
  out give a balance shown beside the one in force, saving nothing and auditing nothing.
  An Admin saves the settings tried once an estimate is agreed.
- **The long reports worked out ahead.** The coverage report, the renewal worklist, the
  renewal alert and the capacity balance are worked out each night and when the server
  starts, and shared by every session; an Admin's Check kept reports works each out
  anew and names any that had fallen out of step.

## Changed

- **The capacity report's support weight is in its parts**: the items, the places and
  the circuits, with how many counted at a default and were raised to a minimum, and
  the demand of each region holds the places and circuits within it.
- **The capacity report is kept** as the coverage report is, until a change or the next
  day.
- **The long reports are worked out ahead** just after midnight and when the server
  starts, by a job of the server's own, `long reports`, in place of 0.7.0's `renewal
  alert`. The coverage report and the worklist asked with and without the warning
  period are one kept answer.

## Additional Notes

- Every component is at its latest production release. Nothing is held back.
- **Upgrading from 0.7.0 runs migration `0010`**, which adds the tables of circuits and
  the weight of a place, and changes nothing that is held. The six weights are blank
  and change no count until an Admin sets one. The way back is the backup, as
  [Install and operate](../install.md#from-070-to-080) says.
- P-139 to P-142 and P-145 to P-157 are the particulars of this release, each built as
  it stands with the owner's answers, and the readings the two reviews settled, in its
  row of [Decisions](../decisions.md).
- **For those who build `hirdir`:** the server was built in three lanes at once from one
  schema, the lead building the report of what circuits cost and the kept answers;
  it was reviewed twice by readers who had not written it, the second time on the
  corrections; and the web UI was built in two lanes, each running its own browser
  scenarios.
- Scenarios S01 to S35 and S39 to S50 pass.
- **Timing**, on the 50,000 items of `make fixture` with 2,000 circuits, by the method
  of D-165. Every page of the circuit grid, the record of a circuit and of a group and
  the list of groups meet the target, and the default and the minimum of an item's
  weight cost nothing that can be measured. A kept answer of the coverage report, the
  worklist, the alert or the capacity balance takes 9 to 17 ms, and each is worked out
  at midnight and at start. The report of what circuits cost narrowed by a place, which
  joined every circuit to every place when the timing began, takes 105 to 124 ms at the
  median and counts each circuit once. Still left to the timing of 1.0.0, with their
  numbers in "Timing at scale" in [Architecture](../architecture.md#timing-at-scale):
  the report of what circuits cost whole (267 to 324 ms at the median, 666 to 701 ms at
  the 95th percentile), the capacity report anew with an item's default or minimum set
  (2.5 s), a Try (2.5 s), the check of what is kept (4.3 s), the coverage report anew
  (1.4 s), the summary (2.5 s), and an import of 10,000 rows, not timed again (122 s to
  preview and 168 s to apply, against 30 s and 60 s).
