# hirdir 0.9.12

One fault of the web page mended, and nothing else: the form of a tall row of a table
opens with the button that opened it beside it, and not out of sight above the window.

## Fixes

- **While the form of a row is open, the row's buttons stand at the foot of the row,
  beside the form** (P-278). Under Admin, Settings, Change on a setting whose
  description is long, `busy_response_ms`, `busy_cpu_percent` or
  `reference_file_types`, opened the form beneath the setting's row, as every form of a
  row opens, with the row's own buttons out of sight above the window: such a row is
  taller than what the window has left beside the form. On a window of 1280 by 800,
  with the form's first field in view, the Change button of `busy_response_ms` was 558
  pixels above the top of the window. Now, while its form is open, a row's buttons are
  directly above the form, Change with the mark that it is open, and at the head of
  the row again once the form is closed, where the focus goes back to Change. It holds
  wherever a form opens beneath a row: Settings, Tags, Lifecycle rules, Files, and the
  Files and links of a record. In a row of one line nothing moves. The form itself was
  in view and worked before, and nothing was lost by the fault.

## Additional Notes

- Upgrading from 0.9.11 changes nothing that is held: there is no migration, and the
  schema stays at revision `0015`. See "From 0.9.11 to 0.9.12" in
  [Install and operate](../install.md#from-0911-to-0912).
- A web page that was open while the upgrade ran is the page of 0.9.11 until it is
  loaded again. It says under its menu bar that a new version is installed, with a
  button, Load again.
- Going back to 0.9.11 needs no backup: it is the one `apt install --allow-downgrades`
  command.
- **The descriptions of the settings are left as long as they need to be.** The owner
  chose the button beside the form over shorter descriptions. The scenario that holds
  the form beneath its row tried the first setting and the last, and so met the fault
  only when 0.9.11 added a setting with a long description at the end; it now tries the
  tallest row as well, and fails with the fix taken out.
- **The second process writes no line when it has worked the long reports out**
  (P-279), as in 0.9.11: the owner was asked and wants none. It writes one where
  working ahead failed. `GET /kept-answers`, and Kept reports under Settings, say when
  each report was worked out.
- Every component is at its latest production release. Nothing is held back.
- Scenarios S01 to S35 and S39 to S53 pass.
