# hirdir 0.9.18

Each side of a circuit has its dates, and a circuit's Z side may name the carrier network
it reaches. The country and the state of every address are chosen from lists an Admin
keeps, starting with the Americas, the United States first. And what is typed or pasted
is put into one form: telephone numbers by the patterns an Admin chooses, email
addresses in lower case, and on the web page a person's name in Title Case.

## New

- **Each side of a circuit has its dates** (P-317, P-320). The A side's are
  `a_installed_on`, `a_activated_on` and `a_disconnected_on`, the Z side's
  `z_installed_on`, `z_activated_on` and `z_disconnected_on`; a blank Z date is read as
  the A side's, with the source `a_side`, never copied. The dates of the side `cost_side`
  names decide whether a circuit is in service, the months its cost counts and whether it
  carries its weight, as the owner chose. Each side's dates keep their order on what is
  in force, so a change of an A side's date that would put the Z side's out of order is
  refused at the Z side's field.
- **Carrier networks** (P-319, P-320). A circuit group is a **Network**, a carrier's MPLS
  network, the internet, a Metro Ethernet region or a cloud on-ramp, which may name its
  carrier, or a **Collection**, as every group was until now. A circuit's Z side names a
  location, a network, or neither with its note. A network lists the circuits whose Z
  side names it beside those added by hand, each once, and counts and costs each once; it
  is not retired while a living circuit names it, nor made a collection while any does.
- **Countries and states** (P-318, P-321). Two lists under Admin › Choices, an Admin's to
  change and every role's to read, each a file of the Import page and in Export
  everything: 55 countries of the Americas from the ISO 3166 data of `iso-codes` 4.20.1,
  the United States first and never removed nor recoded, and their 633 subdivisions,
  Puerto Rico, Guam, the US Virgin Islands, American Samoa and the Northern Mariana
  Islands among the United States'. Every address, street and shipping, of a region, a
  site and a location keeps a country's code and a state's code, chosen on the page by
  name, taken by the API, a file and the CLI by code or by name, and answered with
  `country_name` and `state_name`. A blank country is read as the United States where the
  address has another field. `GET /places/unlisted-addresses`, and the page beneath the
  two lists, name every place whose address holds a value no list holds.
- **Input formats** (P-293, P-322). Three settings: `phone_formats`, the patterns a
  telephone number is written in, tried in order (`###-###-####` and `+1 (###) ###-####`
  offered, an Admin's own beside them), empty until an Admin chooses one, as the owner
  answered; `email_lower_case`, on; and `title_case_names`, on, the web page's alone. The
  server applies the first two to a contact's numbers and email however they come in, and
  to whom to ask; a number with a letter in it, or one no pattern fits, is kept as typed,
  and one already in a pattern's form is kept as it is. The page tidies a box when the
  person leaves it or pastes into it, Title Case raising the first letter of each word of
  a person's name and lowering none, a person's correction standing. A file's preview
  shows the value a format writes beside the file's, `as_in_file`.

## Changed

- **The dates of a circuit are renamed** (P-320, migration `0019`): `installed_on`,
  `activated_on` and `disconnected_on` are `a_installed_on`, `a_activated_on` and
  `a_disconnected_on` in the API, the files, the filters and sorts, and the CLI, with no
  alias. A script, a file or a saved filter that uses the old names must change.
- **A circuit's groups** include the network its Z side names, marked `z_side`; the
  circuits file's `groups` column holds only the groups a circuit was added to by hand.
  The circuits file and the grid's export write the Z side's dates in force, each with
  its source, `z_installed_on_source` and the like (`own` or `a_side`).
- **The country before the state** in every address form, so that the state's list
  follows the country chosen.
- **Export everything loads whole into an empty inventory where every address is in the
  lists** (P-321): a place whose address holds a country or a state no list holds is
  refused at that field, as the owner chose, until a person chooses the value on the
  unlisted addresses.

## Fixes

- The package test made the service account of its upgrade check with
  `is_service_account`, which 0.9.17 refuses; it gives `kind` now.
- A test that loaded the examples with the application's worker running could have its
  load stopped by the worker; it stops the worker first, as `hirdir admin load-examples`
  is run with the service stopped.

## Additional Notes

- **Upgrade.** Migration `0019` renames the A side's dates in place, adds the Z side's
  and its network, gives every circuit group the kind Collection, makes and loads the two
  lists, and turns into codes the countries and states held that match them exactly, a
  name or a code without regard to case, or `USA`, `U.S.`, `U.S.A.` and `United States of
  America`, with an audit event for each place it changes; anything else is kept as typed
  and listed for a person to choose. Nothing held is tidied by a format. See
  [From 0.9.17 to 0.9.18](../install.md#from-0917-to-0918).
- **This release's test** (P-305, an ordinary release): `make check`, `make acceptance`,
  the browser scenarios on the host of every spec file changed since 0.9.17, one
  `make deb`, and of the package test `--upgrade` from 0.9.17, `--failed-upgrade`,
  `--fresh` and `--browser-check`.
- **Reviews.** Two of the server and one of the web in a browser found what was mended
  before the build: a file that copied an inherited Z date down as the circuit's own, a
  telephone format that wrote a number another way the second time, and on the page one
  that dropped a code of a country typed after a bracket; a country compared by its text
  rather than its code; changes of the lists that did not wait for the writer's place; a
  circuit's Z location let go of while a person looked through the carrier networks, and
  an address's state lost when its country was changed and changed back, among others.
- **Next, 1.0.0**: timing at 50,000 items, the import of 10,000 rows within its targets,
  the backup timer and restore, the upgrade test from the release just before, an
  accessibility pass, and the documentation completed.
