# hirdir 0.9.5

Two reviews of the web UI, for the work a network operations team does with it every
day: finding an item and what is near it, adding an item, changing an item, finding a
site or a location, the items and the contacts of a location, and counts. Each change
was measured in a browser before and after, on the fictional examples and on a
fictional inventory of some 47,000 items. Nothing changes in the API, in the database
or in what is held.

## New

- **Find an item, from every page.** The menu bar has a box, Find an item: a part of a
  name, a host name, a serial number, an asset tag, an address or a MAC address, and
  Enter, opens the list of items that hold it, minor items among them. Finding a switch
  by its host name took the name of the field and its operator,
  `hostname:contains:…`, 26 keys; it takes the words.
- **Plain words in the filter box of every list.** Text that holds no condition of the
  short form is taken as words: a record matches when each word is held by one of the
  fields its list is known by, an item's name, host name, serial number, asset tag and
  address, a contact's name and email, a catalog entry's PID and model, and so for each
  list. A condition of the short form works as it did. Text that is neither is answered
  with that list's own example, and a list never shows every record under text it did
  not understand. Owners has the box as the other lists do.
- **Find a place.** Geography has a box, Find a place: sites by their name, code and
  region, and locations by their name and code and those of the site they are in, so
  that `S077 floor 2` finds that floor. Up to ten of each are listed by their path, each
  a link, with how many more match. A rack inside a site took three clicks and some
  6,600 pixels of scrolling; it takes its name.
- **Add another like it.** The form of a new item has a second button, Create and add
  another like it. The item is made and the page says so, with a link to it; the form
  stays open with the catalog entry, the owner, the operator, the location, the planned
  location, the state and the tags of the last one, and its serial number, asset tag,
  name, host name and MAC address blank, the keyboard in the serial number. After the
  first, Enter in a box makes the next, so a scanner's Enter does not end the run.
- **From an item to what is near it.** On the page of an item: beside its location and
  its site, a link, items here, to the list of items filtered to that place; among its
  first facts, its lifecycle state, disposition and intent; and under its name, links to
  the sections of the page.
- **Change, from a fact to its field.** Beside the catalog entry, the owner, the
  operator, the location, the lifecycle state and the comments at the top of an item's
  page, Change moves to that field of the form and puts the keyboard in it, for a person
  who may change the item. The location was shown 736 pixels from the top and changed
  6,870 pixels from it.
- **Buttons in reach.** Save and Undo of a long form stay at the foot of the window
  while the form is in it. A form opened by a button New comes into the window with the
  keyboard in its first field: on Contracts it opened out of sight above the window.
- **What a page answers stays in view.** That a change was made, or why it was refused,
  stays at the top of the window while the page is scrolled past its place. After Save
  at the foot of an item's page, "The item was saved." was some 7,000 pixels out of
  sight.
- **Where each item is, in the list.** Site and Location are shown from the start, and
  the columns shown from the start stand as Name, PID, Serial number, State, Health,
  Site and Location, which fit a window 1280 pixels wide, then Operator, Owner, Vendor,
  Disposition, Tags and LDoS. Expected online is in the column chooser, where each
  person turns any column on and off; the layout is kept.
- **A place's contacts first, one click to call or write.** On the page of a region, a
  site or a location, what is in force there, its contacts first, stands above its files
  and links, and the email and the number of each contact are links.
- **A site with no location, where a location is asked for.** An item is put at a
  location, never at a site. Where a location is searched for and none matches, the
  field names the sites the same words find, each with a link to its page, where a
  location is made, and the page of a site with no location says so.
- **Home sooner, and counts that open.** Home shows its totals from the plain summary
  and no longer waits for the summary by operator: on the large inventory the first
  total came after 5.4 to 6.9 seconds and comes after 2.9 to 3.4. Each total of health,
  on Home and on the summary, is a link to the items with that health. A count written
  in a sentence has the separator of thousands, `34,778 items`.
- **The Scoreboard at size.** It begins with links to its tables; a table of more than
  twenty rows shows twenty, as sorted, and a button, Show all; the operators involved
  with a group or a site are a number with the list behind it. With 460 groups and 121
  sites the page was 110 windows tall and is 6.4.
- **The audit trail links each change to its record's page**, where it has one.

## Fixes

- **A picker asks again.** A field that finds a record by what is typed sent no request
  when Enter was pressed or the same text typed again, so a location made a moment
  before in another tab was not found until the text changed, the page was loaded again
  or the person signed in again. It searches again on Enter, on the same text typed
  again and on coming back to the tab; the lists of choices a page reads when it opens
  are read again on coming back to the tab too.
- **The form of an item's standing offers what its state allows.** It offered every
  disposition and both intents, and the server refused those the lifecycle rule of the
  state does not allow: on a switch in production, three of five dispositions and one of
  two intents. It offers what the rule allows; the server judges as before.
- **A list on a busy server went quiet.** A filter changed twice while the list was
  still being read left the grid waiting on reads it had let go: it asked for no more
  rows and showed no count. Such a read is now ended properly.
- **A contact named in an agreement was said to be assigned nowhere.** The page of a
  contact lists the places, owners and operators it is assigned to; a contact named only
  in an agreement, a contract, a vendor or a circuit is now said to be named there, with
  the number, not to be assigned nowhere.
- **Pages that scrolled sideways.** Capacity was 1,639 pixels wide in a window of 1,280,
  and the table of past imports wider than its column. A form opened under a row of a
  table comes wholly into the window, its button to save with it.

## Additional Notes

- Upgrading from 0.9.4 changes nothing that is held: there is no migration, and the
  schema stays at revision `0012`. See "From 0.9.4 to 0.9.5" in
  [Install and operate](../install.md#from-094-to-095).
- Going back to 0.9.4 needs no backup: it is the one `apt install --allow-downgrades`
  command.
- **A layout of the list of items kept before 0.9.5 stays as it was**, with the columns
  it had: sorting or filtering the list once keeps a layout. Reset layout and filters,
  under the list, brings the new columns and their order.
- P-182 to P-197 are this release's, each in its row of [Decisions](../decisions.md)
  with what was measured. They were chosen by two surveys of the web UI in a browser,
  built, and read by reviewers who had not built them; what the reviewers found was
  mended before the release, among it a box in the menu bar that opened every item under
  text it could not read, and links of the Scoreboard that lost the path prefix of an
  installation behind nginx.
- Left for a later release, each seen and measured: a catalog entry is chosen from a
  list of every entry, which typing does not search; a model that is not in the catalog
  cannot be added from the form of a new item; a list does not keep its place when a
  person opens a row and comes back; the page of a contact does not list the agreements,
  contracts, vendors and circuits that name it; a location is not found by the name of a
  region above its site's own; the boxes of why an item has each value stand open and
  make its page long; and the total of health on Home is a link whose filter the web
  builds from the settings, until the summary answers the filter of what it counts.
- On an inventory of some 47,000 items the Scoreboard takes some 9 seconds and the
  summary by operator 4: the reports at that size go to the timing of 1.0.0.
- Twenty browser scenarios are new, in three files, one for each review and lane.
  They add no numbered scenario, as a release of corrections adds none.
- The package test carries one `SKIP`: with no migration there is none to make fail, so
  that check is skipped and says so. The pass upgrades and goes back all the same.
- Every component is at its latest production release. Nothing is held back.
- Scenarios S01 to S35 and S39 to S53 pass.
