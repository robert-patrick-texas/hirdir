# hirdir 0.2.1

A correction to 0.2.0, made from the answers to its open questions. Every time is now
shown in one time zone, the same for everybody, and support weight is kept to two
decimal places like money.

## Changed

- **One time zone for everybody.** Every time is shown in the application's time zone,
  with the zone named: `2026-01-31 14:05:09 EST`. It no longer follows the zone of the
  reader's own machine, so two people who speak of a time mean the same moment. The zone
  is the `time_zone` setting, America/New_York until an Admin changes it under Admin,
  Settings. This holds for every page, for a filter on a time, for a time that is typed
  in, and for signing in at the command line.
- **Backups are named in that time zone**, with its offset from UTC:
  `hirdir-20261111T210000-0500.db`. Backups that 0.2.0 made keep their names, and the
  newest fourteen are still the ones kept, whichever way they were named.
- **Support weight has two decimal places**, as money and percentages have. A weight
  that 0.2.0 stored with three is rounded to the nearest hundredth when the package is
  upgraded: `0.125` becomes `0.13`.
- **Text is compared as if it were all in lower case**, in a filter and in a sort.

## Additional Notes

- Every component is at its latest production release. Nothing is held back.
- Upgrading from 0.2.0 takes the schema from revision `0002` to `0003`. See
  [Install and operate](../install.md#upgrade).
- `make package-test` now upgrades from 0.2.0 and carries a catalog entry and an item
  across. When the package of the previous release is not at hand it skips the upgrade
  and says so, where 0.2.0 stopped with a failure.
- The check of an upgrade whose schema change fails is skipped for this release, and
  says so: it works by putting a table in the way of one the new version creates, and
  0.2.1 creates none. The 0.2.0 run of that check, and a test of a migration that fails
  part-way, still cover it.
- The questions that 0.2.0 left open are answered and are now D-49 to D-64 in
  [Decisions](../decisions.md). A count includes an item that has no lifecycle state,
  because it may be in the team's hands; once a state is given, the state decides.
- No scenario of the [build plan](../build-plan.md) is added. S01 to S11 pass.
